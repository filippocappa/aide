Aide privacy policy

Aide is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It runs on the owner's own server.

Google user data

  • Aide reads the owner's Gmail (scope gmail.readonly) only to answer the owner's own requests, such as searching or summarising mail.
  • Aide sends mail as the owner (scope gmail.send) only after the owner has seen and confirmed each message.
  • Mail content is sent to the AI model provider to produce answers, and is kept only in the chat history on the owner's server. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising or model training.
  • Aide's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
